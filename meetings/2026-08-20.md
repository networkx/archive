## Topics (updated this week)
- For graph similarity, we have OED, SimRank and Panther. [Issue 8829](https://github.com/networkx/networkx/issues/8823) proposes to implement simrank_star as an improvement on simrank, and to include a "method" parameter leading to use of an exact solution rather than an iterative approach. The SimRank* paper is 2019 with 52 citations. The exact method's paper is 90 days old (no citations). Contributor has 1 successful networkx PR changing MIS output from list to set.
    - Should we encourage a PR, or ask them to try their own repo?
